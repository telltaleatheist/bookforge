Almost certainly batch-shape dependence: llama.cpp's fused kernels reduce in an
order that depends on how many sequences share the batch, and `cache_prompt`
means a slot's prefix may or may not be recomputed. Nothing here is sampling —
top_k is 1 throughout. It shows up as a near-tie flipping, e.g. a straight
apostrophe vs a curly one at the end of a quotation.
